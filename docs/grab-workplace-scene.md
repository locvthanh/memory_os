# The Green Court — Tony's Grab workplace scene

Scene id: `grab-workplace` · Blender source: `blender_models/GrabCourtyard.blend`
(generated by `blender_models/grab_scenegen/`) · 25 loci.

## Concept

A stylized Grab-green modern office laid out as a **hex courtyard with six wings**.

The rule the building encodes, and the only one you need to remember:

> **The centre is what matters now. The wings are where the work lives.**

Everything that moves week to week — priorities, the todo board, the site-lead
view — is in the court, visible from every wing mouth. Everything that is
durable — the six areas, their charters, their meeting rooms — is out in a wing
with a door you walk through. You can stand in the middle and see all six areas
at once; that is the site lead's job made into architecture.

Six wings, because there are six things: one org you belong to, one site you
lead, four teams you manage.

| Direction | Wing | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| N (90°) | **Tech Infra Concourse** | the org you're a member of |
| NE (30°) | **VN R&D Site Floor** | the site you lead |
| SE (−30°) | **Finapps** | team you manage |
| S (270°) | **UCM** | team you manage |
| SW (210°) | **Temporal** | team you manage |
| NW (150°) | **Web Platforms** | team you manage |

Each wing is identical in shape and different in content: a green portal arch
with the wing's name, a team room with desks and a whiteboard, one **plinth
carrying that area's peg object**, a glass partition, and the wing's own
**meeting room** at the far end with a named plate over the door.

The six *corners* of the court (the hex vertices, which sit between the wings)
each carry one piece of court furniture. So the tour spirals clockwise:
corner, wing, corner, wing — you never walk the same kind of space twice in a row.

Countries are deliberately not spatial. VN, SG and India are people, not rooms,
and they'd have fought the wings for the same axis.

## The peg per wing

The thing on the plinth is what makes the wing stick:

- **Tech Infra** — a scale model of this whole building on the plinth ("THE
  BUILDING ITSELF"). Infra carries everything, including the room you're
  standing in.
- **VN R&D** — a hiring board: filled green tiles and hollow outlines.
- **Finapps** — a balance: a coin stack on one pan, a receipt tape on the other.
- **UCM** — a switchboard: one jack in, many coloured cords fanning out to
  little screens.
- **Temporal** — an escapement: a ratchet wheel, a pawl, and a tape spool.
  Never loses its place, however long it is stopped.
- **Web Platforms** — a shopfront facade standing on scaffolding, nothing
  behind it.

The ring closes — 25 loops back to 1 — so it reads in either direction and
recall isn't locked to one direction of travel.

**Loci 2, 6 and 10 are deliberately blank pegs.** The obelisk's five slots, the
three todo columns and the balcony desk carry no text: the priorities live in
Tony's head and change weekly, and baking this quarter's into geometry would
make the scene stale by design. The *slots* are the mnemonic; what goes in them
is recalled, not read.

**Locus 22, The Open Door**, is this scene's antilibrary: a free-standing door
in the court that opens onto light and nothing else — what you don't yet know
about your own org.

## Geometry

Blender Z-up, metres, building centred on the origin.

- Court: hexagon, apothem 11 m (the wing mouths), circumradius 12.7 m
  (the corners). Six columns at the corners carrying a ring beam at 6 m.
  Open to the sky.
- Wings: r = 11 → 29 m, 9 m wide, walls 3.6 m, `ROOF_` slab on top (the prefix
  skips shadow casting in the viewer). Team room r 12 → 20.5, glass partition
  with a centreline doorway at 20.5, meeting room 20.5 → 29.
- Locus radii per wing: gate sign 13.0, peg 16.5, meeting table 25.0.
- Court furniture on the six corner rays at r ≈ 9–10.6; the balcony deck at
  height 4.0 m.

## Camera

Radial anchoring with a **negative** `anchorDistance` (interior scene — the
camera sits on the court side of each locus and looks outward). Per-locus
overrides: −6.0 at wing gates, −4.2 at pegs, −4.0 in meeting rooms. The centre
obelisk has no radial direction of its own, so it uses `anchorFrom` a point due
south to put the camera in the court looking north past the obelisk to the
Tech Infra arch.

## Possible follow-ups

- A portal from the Tech Infra model-of-the-building peg back to the hub.
- If the team set changes, a wing can be renamed without touching geometry —
  the sign, the plate and the peg are the only per-wing content.
- A seventh wing is buildable (the hex becomes a heptagon) but the six-fold
  symmetry is load-bearing for recall; prefer re-labelling a wing.
